Our Identity & Heritage

Three Decades of Advisory Excellence.

The Prime Real Estates was founded with a singular conviction: real estate decisions should be guided by thoughtful counsel, empirical market understanding, and unwavering loyalty to the client.

Our Core Philosophy

"To simplify real estate decisions in India through trusted local expertise, personalised advisory, and carefully considered opportunities."

In a market often characterised by aggressive brokers and conflicting incentives, The Prime Real Estates serves as an objective advisor. We do not push listings; we evaluate every property against the precise strategic, financial, and lifestyle parameters set by our clients.

Our clients include foreign diplomatic missions, multinational corporations relocating leadership teams from Japan, South Korea, Europe, and the US, as well as family offices managing multi-state real estate assets.
